      Job
      
              
            
      
            
      
              
      
            
      
          
      
          
      Each job runs in Rust as a Tokio task and counts from 1 to the number given, pausing between counts. Jobs beyond the "At once" limit wait in the queue. Kill stops a queued job before it starts, or a running job at its next pause. "Late by" shows how much later than asked the pauses ended, which rises when the machine is busy.

      How to use the app
      
              
      
                
      
                  
      Moving between pages
      
                  
      Choose a page from the menu. On the Options tab, Menu layout switches between tabs across the top (Horizontal) and a menu down the left side (Vertical). In the vertical menu, the button at the top left contracts the menu to icons and expands it again. The app remembers your choice.
      
                  
      Jobs
      
                  
      The Jobs page starts mock jobs that run in Rust as Tokio tasks and count up to a number, pausing between counts. "At once" sets how many run at the same time (1 to 3, also on the Options tab); the rest wait in the queue. Each row shows progress, time spent waiting and running, and how late the pauses ended. Inspect shows a job's details, including its Tokio task, and Kill stops it: before it starts if it is still queued, or at its next pause if it is running. "Fail at step" makes a job fail on purpose, to test error handling.
      
                  
      Errors
      
                  
      The Errors page lists errors and warnings since the app started, newest first, including errors in the page's own scripts. New ones appear at once; while another page is open, a red count on the Errors menu entry shows how many arrived. Copy puts the list on the clipboard, Clear empties it, and Test records a test error. Options, Errors kept, sets how many are kept (10 to 1000, default 100).
      
                  
      Searching
      
                  
      In the vertical menu, type at least three letters into Search, for example "dash" or "theme". Use Up and Down to choose a result and Enter to go straight to it. Escape closes the list. / or Ctrl+K moves to the search box (see Shortcut keys below). When the menu is contracted, the magnifying glass expands it and opens the search.
      
                  
      Themes
      
                  
      Pick a theme from the palette button in the title bar, or from Theme on the Options tab.
      
                  
      The title bar
      
                  
      Drag the title bar to move the window. Drag any edge or corner to resize it. The T button hides the title bar; the small arrow in the top right corner brings it back. Title bar size on the Options tab makes the bar and its buttons larger.
      
                  
      Rearranging
      
                  
      Drag a card or panel by its grip, or focus the grip and press Up, Down, Home or End. Each page has a button to reset its order.
      
                  
      Panels and tables
      
                  
      Panels remember whether they are open or closed. Each table has its own search box that filters its rows as you type.
      
                  
      Options
      
                  
      Change settings, then press Save. Revert puts back the values from before your changes.
      
                  
      Accessibility
      
                  
      The Options tab has Text size, High contrast, Larger click targets, Underline links, Strong focus ring and Reduce motion. Each one takes effect as soon as you press Save.
      
                  
      Tray icon
      
                  
      Right-click the tray icon to show, hide or quit the app.
      
                  
      Settings files
      
                  
      Your settings are kept in config.json in the folder shown below. options.json lists the controls on the Options tab, search.json lists the places the search can find, and keys.json holds the shortcut keys.
